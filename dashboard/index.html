What this board is

Every row is a protocol whose sealed snapshot entry records no token: no market cap, no CoinGecko id, no symbol. The ranked board carries all of them, not only the house watchlist. Protocols that already trade are kept apart on the tab; their market cap divided by TVL is what sets each category's multiple.

What it is not

DefiLlama's record is not proof. A protocol can carry a token its record does not show, and this board would still list it. The implied value is a model output from three inputs, not a forecast, a promise or investment advice; the farm plans are steps for collecting a possible launch, with their costs and times stated.

the model, in full

Implied value from three inputs

implied FDV = TVL × benchmark multiple × growth multiplier × maturity multiplier

1 · Benchmark multiple

The median market-cap-to-TVL multiple of the live-token protocols in the same category, from this snapshot alone. A category needs at least five live peers; otherwise the whole market's median stands in. The multiple is clamped to the stated range and every row names the peers behind it.

2 · Growth multiplier

The protocol's seven-day TVL change, clamped and scaled by the growth weight. A protocol whose TVL grew is worth more than an identical one whose TVL shrank, with the same rule for both.

3 · Maturity multiplier

Days since DefiLlama began tracking the protocol with no token recorded, against a four-year cap. A protocol that has run longer without a token gets the higher multiplier; an unknown listing date takes the median pre-token age and the row says so.

What is excluded

Live-token protocols, CEX entries, and wrapped, staked, bridged or versioned products of a token that already trades. The watchlist is the floor: every watchlist slug is scored, and the board adds the protocols the list leaves out.

No slug is special-cased: one function scores every protocol. The snapshot carries no ranking, no implied value and no farm plan; deriving them is this entry's work.
